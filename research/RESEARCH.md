# Research and design decisions

Checked 10 October 2026. This record distinguishes direct browser observation, primary documentation and our own design judgments. References informed principles; their source code, models, media, typography and identities are not reused.

## Camera subjects and mobile pacing follow-up

Re-read the primary City policies and inspected the City's [proposed street map](https://vancouver.ca/images/cov/content/nefc-after-map.jpg) and [6C sketch](https://vancouver.ca/images/cov/content/nefc-sub-area-6c-sketch.jpg). The camera previously varied distance while continuing to aim near the same district centre. The revised views follow specific places within the existing geographic scene:

| Subject                           | Primary context                                                                                                                                                                             | Camera decision                                                                                                     |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Site / viaduct edge               | [City streetscape policy](https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/streetscape-design.aspx) explains how the viaducts bypass the area and interrupt its connection to downtown.       | Approach the 6C land and infrastructure together, with BC Place and Rogers Arena retaining orientation.             |
| Dunsmuir / Carrall                | [City urban design policy](https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/urban-design.aspx) identifies two principal city-to-park connections.                                             | Rise over their relationship to the site's northern edge before following Georgia toward the water.                 |
| Georgia Landing                   | The same policy identifies 6C at Georgia Street meeting False Creek; the [811 Carrall proposal](https://www.shapeyourcity.ca/811-carrall-st) includes a public plaza at its foot.           | A closer, higher-angle waterfront view, with route drawing reaching the landing during that view.                   |
| Creekside Park extension          | [City park design principles](https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/park-design.aspx) locate the extension north of Science World and emphasize shoreline habitat and connections. | Move east to the park and shoreline, with Science World as the recognizable foreground anchor.                      |
| Neighbourhood / park relationship | The public 6C proposal includes mixed uses and delivery of the park extension; its application is still on hold.                                                                            | Orbit toward the low conceptual zones and their park frontage, then return to the established exploration overview. |

These are editorial framing decisions inferred from policy, not new planning geometry. Existing geography, future overlays, material treatment, typography and story copy remain intact. No reference images were added to the assets.

Phone travel is now 15 small-viewport heights instead of 21. Smooth raised-cosine pacing allocates more distance around the five close views and less between them. Native momentum remains available; there are no captured gestures, mandatory stops or scroll snaps. The same reversible position mapping handles Continue, playback and resizing. Desktop scroll scoring remains linear. The world transformation score is aligned with the views so the routes arrive during reconnection and the park is legible during its close-up. Long labels are hidden when their full measured width cannot fit beside an anchor.

## Project and brand grounding

[Concord Pacific Place](https://www.concordpacificplace.com/) describes eight of nine neighbourhoods as built, with Concord Landing remaining. The [City application page](https://www.shapeyourcity.ca/811-carrall-st) says the 811 Carrall / Sub-area 6C application is **on hold**. Area-plan policy and a site rezoning are different evidence classes. The concept promises neither a delivery date nor approved architecture.

Reviewed official [Concord Pacific](https://www.concordpacific.com/), [Metrotown](https://www.concordmetrotown.com/), [Brentwood](https://www.concordbrentwood.com/), [PIANO](https://concordpiano.ca/), [Park George](https://www.parkgeorge.com/) and [commercial project material including ARC](https://commercial.concordpacific.com/all-buildings?country=canada). These projects have distinct identities within a shared developer relationship. Our inference: Concord Landing can have its own world without reproducing the corporate logo. The original shoreline mark, concrete / white palette and controlled connection blue serve this place. Green is reserved for landscape.

## Geographic pipeline

- City LiDAR 2022 was investigated first. Individual LAS tiles can be very large. Four relevant tiles returned HTTP 403 to bounded requests; no full-city download or access workaround was attempted.
- Accessible [GeoBC LidarBC](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/data/geographic-data-services/topographic-data/lidarbc) DSM / DEM rasters provide a practical alternative. Twenty 2025 one-metre tiles intersect the cropped district context. Source raster metadata supplies EPSG:6653; the horizontal CRS is EPSG:3157. DSM minus DEM uses the same vertical datum, so relative heights do not mix City and provincial vertical systems.
- City footprints, shoreline, roads and parks are cropped to `[-123.144, 49.264, -123.088, 49.297]`. This is approximately 4.1 × 3.7 km around downtown / False Creek, not all of Vancouver. Local scene coordinates use metres around `[-123.109, 49.275]` with north along negative Z.
- Generalized roof height is the 80th percentile of valid DSM-minus-DEM samples inside each footprint. Large roofs are split into coarse eight-metre grid / height bands, clipped to their outlines, with a continuous eight-metre podium. Ground elevations are retained as metadata; the cinematic base is flattened. This is deliberately not a survey model.
- 4,624 of 4,681 footprints have usable samples. The remaining 57 use an explicit nine-metre fallback. Dataset dates differ: 2002 shoreline, 2015 footprints, 2022 orthophoto, 2025 height surface. New construction, changed footprints and vegetation contamination remain possible.
- City orthophoto tiles are cropped and exported as local WebP textures: 2,609 × 2,358 desktop and a smaller 1,536-pixel version. Tile hashes, source CRS, bounds and algorithm are retained in `public/data/context.json` / `orthophoto.json`. Raw rasters and intermediate files are ignored.
- BC Place and Science World are original simplified landmark constructions over mapped context. Future park, paths and translucent zones are original policy illustrations, not approved project geometry.

Download safeguards include disk-space checks, per-file limits, a tile-count limit, a geographic crop and limited concurrent image requests. Python dependencies live in `.venv`; there were no administrator, driver, firewall or system configuration changes.

The mobile composition has its own continuous camera distance and calm text area, with reduced detail / DPR. It is a guided story, not a miniature desktop viewer. The 2D alternative preserves sources, editorial progression, comparison and relationships using local aerial geography. Sound was omitted: the experience should first succeed through place and motion, with no licensing or autoplay burden.

## Interpretation limits

Physical phones, Safari / Firefox and reference-site GPU performance were not measured. Planning status can change. Historical basemaps, approximate height bands and original future overlays must remain clearly labelled when the system evolves.
